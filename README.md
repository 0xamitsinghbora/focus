## What is Focus?

Focus is a personal desktop helper that sits quietly on your computer and helps you understand where your day goes.

Instead of waiting until the evening to guess how you spent your time, Focus works with you throughout the day:

- **Friendly Check-in Popups**: Every 20 to 40 minutes, a small, quiet prompt asks what you've been working on. You can type a quick note, link it to a goal, or click Snooze if you are in the flow.
- **Always-in-Sight Timer**: A small floating timer rests on your screen or taskbar so you can glance at your session progress anytime without switching windows.
- **Daily Goal Planning**: List what you want to achieve each morning, set simple time targets, and check them off as you complete them.
- **End-of-Day Review**: Wrap up your day with a clear, visual summary of your productive hours, break time, and progress on each goal.
- **Calming Focus Sounds**: Turn on built-in soothing sounds (like gentle rain or white noise) to block out background noise and help you concentrate.

---

## Why is it needed?

Working on a computer makes it easy to lose hours to distractions, meetings, and multitasking. Focus solves the most common daily work challenges:

- **No more wondering "Where did my day go?"**: It is easy to work hard all day and still feel like you accomplished nothing. Focus captures your accomplishments in real time so you always have a clear record of your effort.
- **Gentle accountability**: When you drift off into emails, tabs, or rabbit holes, a periodic check-in brings your attention back to what actually matters—without pressure or guilt.
- **100% Private (No Spying)**: Unlike company monitoring software, Focus **never** watches your screen, never tracks your keystrokes, and never snoops on your browser history. Everything stays on your machine and belongs only to you.
- **Made for everyday people**: You don't need to learn complex project software or manage overwhelming task systems. It is simple, clear, and ready to use right away.

---

## Your Data & Privacy

Your personal notes and logs belong to you:

- **Saved on your computer**: By default, everything is stored strictly on your device. You can use the app completely offline without an internet connection.
- **Optional Cloud Backup**: If you want to keep your notes backed up or use them on another computer, you can easily connect your personal **Google Drive**.

## Quick Start

If you are developing or building Focus from source:

1. Make sure **Node.js** (version 20 or newer) is installed.
2. Open your terminal in the project folder and run:
   ```bash
   npm install
   npm run dev
   ```
3. To preview the GitHub Pages showcase site locally:
   ```bash
   npm run pages:preview
   ```
