Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

No complex multi-tier project tracking or overwhelming setup. Just a simple helper that sits quietly on your computer.

💬

Friendly Check-ins

Every 20 to 40 minutes, a small prompt asks what you're working on. Type a sentence, tag a goal, or snooze if you're in deep flow.

🪟

Floating Timer Widget

A gentle floating pill rests in the corner of your desktop so you can glance at your session progress without switching apps.

🎯

Simple Morning Goals

Jot down 2 or 3 intentions each morning, set simple target hours, and check them off as you complete them throughout the day.

📊

Visual Day Summary

Wrap up your day with a clear, color-coded visual timeline of your productive hours, break intervals, and accomplishments.

🔒

100% Private (No Spying)

Never takes screenshots, never records keystrokes, and never inspects browser tabs. All data stays strictly in your local SQLite.

🎧

Calming Ambient Audio

Built-in gentle rain, brown noise, and ocean soundscapes mask room chatter and background noise when you need deep focus.
